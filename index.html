How it works

One simple flow from the job site to your social accounts. Nothing is ever posted without your approval.

01 · Upload

Snap and send

After a job, the crew uploads photos and clips from their phone in under a minute.

02 · Create

AI builds the posts

We pick the best shots and produce photos, before-and-after videos, slideshows and reels with captions in English or Spanish.

03 · Approve

One tap to approve

The owner reviews each post on their phone, edits the text if needed, and approves.

04 · Publish

Posted on schedule

Approved posts wait in a queue and are published automatically at the times the business picks.

What we build

Software that a busy owner can run without a developer.

  • Fast, bilingual business websites with estimate request forms that go straight to the owner.
  • AI-generated social content from real job photos and videos, in the business's own brand colors and fonts.
  • A publishing queue that posts approved content to the business's own accounts on its schedule.
  • A simple portal for owners and crews that works on any phone.

Connected accounts

Businesses connect their own accounts from inside our portal. They can disconnect at any time, and we only post what the owner has approved.

Facebook PagesInstagramTikTok

TikTok

The business owner signs in with TikTok and grants access. We use the account's basic profile to show which account posts will go to, and TikTok's posting permission to publish the videos the owner approved, with the visibility, comment, duet and stitch settings the owner chose.

Facebook and Instagram

The owner signs in with Facebook and picks the Page and its linked Instagram account. We use that access only to publish the business's approved posts to those accounts.

Your data stays yours

Access tokens are encrypted, each business's data is kept separate, and we never sell data or use it for advertising. Read our Privacy Policy.
